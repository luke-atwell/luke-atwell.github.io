Hexagon AS1 Laser Scanner

Hexagon AS1 laser scanner

Project Overview

The project involved the development of an extension handle for a Hexagon AS1 laser scanner. The design needed to provide the required structural performance while keeping weight low and allowing the scanner to be operated remotely.

The project covered the full development process, from initial concept generation through CAD development and finite element analysis to physical prototyping and evaluation.

01 — Concept Development

Exploring the design space

Multiple concepts were developed and evaluated before progressing to detailed CAD. The design process considered functionality, structural requirements, material selection, manufacturability and assembly.

02 — Design & Analysis

Developing the final design

The selected concept was developed through multiple SolidWorks iterations. CAD modelling and finite element analysis were used to refine the geometry and investigate structural performance, while also considering manufacturing and assembly.

Detailed CAD development
Engineering analysis and design refinement
03 — Prototyping

From CAD to physical prototype

The final design was manufactured and assembled as a physical prototype. The system incorporated a Bowden cable operated trigger linkage actuated using a custom 3D-printed ergonomic motorcycle clutch lever.

Physical prototype
Completed prototype and mechanical actuation

Engineering Approach

CAD development was carried out iteratively, with changes made to improve manufacturability and assembly as the design progressed.

Carbon fibre was used where weight reduction was particularly valuable, while aluminium provided suitable structural and manufacturing characteristics for other components.

Finite element analysis was used to assess the structural response of the design and inform subsequent design decisions.
