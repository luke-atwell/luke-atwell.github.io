Dissertation Project

Composite Extension Handle for the Hexagon AS1 Laser Scanner

Full lifecycle development of a composite aluminium and carbon fibre extension handle for a Hexagon AS1 laser scanner.

The project involved concept development, CAD design, finite element analysis, material selection, physical prototyping and development of a mechanical trigger linkage.

View Full Dissertation

Project Overview

The project involved developing an extension handle that could improve the usability of the laser scanner while maintaining appropriate structural performance and low weight.

Multiple SolidWorks CAD iterations were developed with consideration given to machining, assembly and material selection. ANSYS finite element analysis was then used to investigate the structural behaviour of the design before physical prototyping.

The final prototype incorporated a Bowden cable operated trigger linkage actuated using a custom 3D-printed ergonomic motorcycle clutch lever.
